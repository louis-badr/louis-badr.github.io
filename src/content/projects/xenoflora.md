An interactive olfactory installation, and the first specimen of Cosmoflore, a fictional house of xenobotanical perfumery. It imagines a future where alien fragrances are reconstructed without taking the organisms from their native environments.

Visitors lean over a glass bell jar to smell a bioplastic flower that levitates on electromagnets. A capacitive sensor detects their approach and brings the plant to life with light, a projection of its habitat and a naturalist's voice.

Made with Mariana Tamashiro, Geetanjali Minsky and perfumer Edward Zhaozheng Liu, and exhibited at ARTeFAB (Fab26, MIT Media Lab, July 2026).
