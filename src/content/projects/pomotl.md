PomoTL is a Pomodoro timer with a horizontal timeline for scheduling work sessions and breaks. Sessions can be resized, tagged and reordered directly on the timeline. An optional AI assistant can plan the day from a written request and modify the schedule as needed.

The app includes a tagged to-do list, an interstitial journal, daily focus goals and shared rooms for synchronizing sessions with others. YouTube videos can play in the background for music or ambient sound. A macOS companion displays the timer in the menu bar and provides quick access to the app and session notifications.
